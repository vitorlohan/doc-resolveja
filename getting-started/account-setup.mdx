Before you dive into managing tickets, take a few minutes to set up your Resolve account. A complete profile helps your teammates identify you in ticket assignments and comments, while the right notification and timezone settings ensure you receive accurate, timely updates as your team's work progresses.

## Initial account setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in for the first time">
    Open the invitation email sent by your Resolve administrator and click **Accept Invitation**. You'll be prompted to create a password for your account.

    - Your password must be at least 10 characters and include at least one uppercase letter, one number, and one special character.
    - After setting your password, you'll be signed in automatically and taken to the Kanban board.

    <Note>
      Invitation links expire after 72 hours. If your link has expired, ask your Resolve administrator to resend the invitation from the **Team Management** settings page.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your display name and avatar">
    Your display name and avatar appear on every ticket you create or are assigned to, making it easy for teammates to identify you at a glance.

    1. Click your **avatar** (or initials icon) in the top-right corner of the screen.
    2. Select **Profile & Settings** from the dropdown menu.
    3. On the **Profile** tab, enter your **Display Name** — this is how your name will appear across Resolve.
    4. Click **Upload Avatar** to add a profile photo. Supported formats are JPG and PNG, up to 5 MB.
    5. Click **Save Changes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your notification preferences">
    Resolve sends notifications when tickets are assigned to you, updated, commented on, or moved to a new status. You control exactly which events trigger an alert.

    1. In **Profile & Settings**, open the **Notifications** tab.
    2. Toggle each notification type on or off based on your preference:

    | Notification type | Description |
    |---|---|
    | **Ticket assigned to me** | Alerts you when a new ticket is assigned to your name |
    | **Status changes** | Notifies you when a ticket you own or watch moves between columns |
    | **New comments** | Sends an alert whenever someone comments on a ticket you're involved with |
    | **Mentions** | Notifies you when a teammate tags you with **@yourname** in a comment |

    3. Click **Save Preferences**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your timezone">
    Resolve uses your timezone to display ticket timestamps, activity logs, and scheduled maintenance windows in your local time.

    1. In **Profile & Settings**, open the **Preferences** tab.
    2. Click the **Timezone** dropdown and search for or select your region and city.
    3. Click **Save Preferences**.

    <Note>
      If your timestamps still look incorrect after saving, try refreshing the page. Changes to timezone settings take effect immediately.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change your password

You can update your password at any time from your account settings.

1. Click your **avatar** in the top-right corner and select **Profile & Settings**.
2. Open the **Security** tab.
3. Enter your **Current Password**, then type and confirm your **New Password**.
4. Click **Update Password**.

<Warning>
  If you forget your current password, sign out and use the **Forgot Password** link on the login page to reset it via email. An administrator cannot retrieve your existing password, only trigger a reset.
</Warning>

## Manage email notification preferences

Beyond the in-app notification toggles, Resolve also delivers alerts by email. You can adjust email delivery separately from in-app alerts.

1. In **Profile & Settings**, open the **Notifications** tab.
2. Scroll to the **Email Notifications** section.
3. Choose your preferred **email frequency**:
   - **Immediate** — Sends an email the moment each triggering event occurs.
   - **Hourly digest** — Bundles all notifications from the past hour into a single email.
   - **Daily digest** — Sends one summary email at a time you specify each day.
4. Confirm the email address shown is correct. To update it, click **Change Email** and follow the verification steps.
5. Click **Save Preferences**.

<Note>
  Disabling email notifications does not disable in-app notifications. You can configure both channels independently to match your preferred way of working.
</Note>

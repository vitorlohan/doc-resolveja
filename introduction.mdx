Resolve is an internal ticketing system built specifically for infrastructure and operations teams. It replaces scattered emails, chat messages, and spreadsheets with a single, structured workflow — giving everyone on your team a clear picture of what needs attention, who owns it, and where it stands right now.

## Who Resolve is for

Resolve is designed for infrastructure and ops teams that need to coordinate maintenance work, track incidents, and keep stakeholders informed without adding overhead. If your team handles server maintenance, network changes, hardware requests, or any recurring operational work, Resolve gives you the structure and visibility to manage it all reliably.

## Key benefits

- **Centralized ticket tracking** — Every infrastructure issue lives in one system, with a full history of updates, comments, and status changes.
- **Kanban visibility** — A visual board shows the state of every ticket at a glance, so nothing gets lost and bottlenecks are obvious.
- **Automatic notifications** — Team members are notified when tickets are assigned, updated, or moved to a new status — no manual follow-ups needed.
- **Team collaboration** — Comment on tickets, tag colleagues, attach files, and keep all context in one thread.

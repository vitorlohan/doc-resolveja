## Get started in three steps

New to Resolve? Follow these steps to go from zero to managing your first ticket in just a few minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Resolve">
    Open Resolve in your browser and sign in with your organization credentials. If this is your first time, check your inbox for an invitation email from your administrator.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first ticket">
    Click **New Ticket** from the Kanban board, fill in the title, description, priority, and assignee, then submit. Your ticket appears instantly in the **To Do** column.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track progress on the Kanban board">
    Drag your ticket across columns as work progresses — from **To Do** to **In Progress** to **Done**. Resolve automatically notifies the assigned team member at each stage.
  </Step>
</Steps>
